How to use Archie

A guide to your feed controls.

Back to posts

  • Help

    Opens this guide. Use Back to posts or the Archie logo to return to the feed.

  • Interact

    Turns post interaction on or off. Switch it on to follow links, play videos, and use the controls inside each post.

    Interaction starts off, so you can grab and drag across cards to scroll on a computer. On mobile, swipe to scroll as usual.

  • Shuffle

    Fades the cards out with a blue glow, brings in up to 14 posts matching your filters, and changes the quote. Unseen posts come first; familiar ones fill any remaining spaces.

    Once you have seen every matching post, each shuffle avoids the previous batch when enough other posts are available. Reloading the feed starts a fresh visit and keeps your filters.
